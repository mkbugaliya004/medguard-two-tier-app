ABOUT THE PLATFORM

Medication safety research prototype

MedGuard is an educational application designed to explore rule-based medication safety assessments for Sotalol, Flecainide and Amiodarone using synthetic patient profiles.

Assessment outputs are generated by the application's current draft rules. They are not clinical recommendations, diagnoses or validated treatment guidance.
